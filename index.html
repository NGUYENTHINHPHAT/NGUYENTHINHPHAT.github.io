FP&A Copilot: Automated Budget Variance Analysis

Automates the budget-vs-actual review that usually takes analysts hours every month-end.

  • Rules engine flags material variances: 10 / 20 / 30% severity tiers, $5K minimum floor, Z-score outliers, 3-period trend detection, BEAT / MISS labels.
  • Retrieval pulls business context to explain why each line moved.
  • Groundedness check maps every cited figure back to source data, so the commentary never invents a number.
